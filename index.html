Fun Facts

1) Halloween began over 2,000 years ago

2) The first Jack-o'-Lanterns were carved out of turnips, not pumpkins

3) A full moon on Halloween only happens about once every 19 years.

4) Americans buy around 600 million pounds of candy every Halloween season

5) The world record for the heaviest pumpkin ever grown weighs nearly 2,820 pounds
